Bolt Bloom: Screw Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Bolt Bloom ("the game") is made by HBN Games. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level, coins and settings on your device only. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this information.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device information and ad interaction data to show, measure and personalize ads. In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. To ask about data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.
